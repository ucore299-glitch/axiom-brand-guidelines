Brand Story & Purpose

Why Axiom exists

The Origin Story

Axiom was founded by Val Infante, a fintech operator who scaled MyBambu to over 1M+ immigrant accounts and served as Chief Growth Officer at Payphone, overseeing $309M in transaction volume and 700K+ users across LATAM.

After years building financial products for underserved communities — from cross-border payments to embedded banking for immigrants — Val saw a pattern: financial institutions are drowning in operational complexity while AI sits on the sidelines as a buzzword, not a business weapon.

Axiom was built to change that. We don't just advise. We build, deploy, and train AI agents and automation systems that financial services companies can actually use — today, not in some theoretical future.

Mission

Transform financial services through AI agents, automation, and training that makes every team faster, smarter, and more human.

Vision

A world where every financial institution — from $1B banks to $10M fintechs — operates with the precision and speed of AI, while every employee has an AI copilot that makes them unstoppable.

Core Beliefs

  • AI is not a feature. It's an operating system. The companies that win won't add AI to their workflows — they'll rebuild their workflows around AI.
  • Operational credibility matters. We've been inside the machine. We know what actually breaks at scale.
  • Training is as critical as technology. The best AI agent is useless if your team doesn't know how to work with it.
  • Fintech + LATAM is the future. The next generation of financial innovation won't come from Silicon Valley alone. It's happening in São Paulo, Mexico City, Bogotá, and Miami.
  • Underserved communities deserve world-class tech. The immigrant sending money home should have AI-powered fraud protection. The small business owner in Ecuador should have AI-driven insights. Technology is not a luxury for the wealthy.

Naming & Tagline

The rationale behind Axiom

Name: AXIOM

Why "Axiom"

  • Definition: A statement or principle that is regarded as being established, accepted, or self-evidently true.
  • Connotation: Mathematical precision. Fundamental truth. The building blocks of logic.
  • Brand fit: AI agents are built on axioms — rules, logic, and systems thinking. Financial services demand axiom-level reliability.
  • Memorability: Short, sharp, distinct. Not generic "AI Solutions" or "Tech Partners."
  • Cultural neutrality: Works equally well in English and Spanish-speaking markets. No awkward translations.

Why One Word — No Suffix

  • Maximum confidence: One word. No ".AI", no "Labs", no "Studio." The brand speaks for itself — like Stripe, like Figma, like Tesla.
  • Category transcendence: Dropping "AI" avoids pigeonholing the brand as just another AI company. Axiom is an operating system for financial services — AI is the means, not the identity.
  • Domain flexibility: axiom.ai, getaxiom.com, axiomhq.com — the one-word name works with any TLD.
  • Unicorn energy: The most iconic companies are one word. Axiom joins that tier from day one.

Typography

Type hierarchy and pairing rules

Type System Overview

Axiom uses a three-typeface system optimized for digital-first applications: Montserrat for display and headlines, Inter for body text and UI, and JetBrains Mono for code, data, and technical content.

DISPLAY / HEADLINES — Montserrat ExtraBold 800

Build AI that works in production

SUBHEADINGS — Montserrat Bold 700

Six service lines. One operating system.

SECTION HEADERS — Montserrat SemiBold 600

Compliance AI, Customer Care AI, Operations AI

BODY TEXT — Inter Regular 400

We build AI agents and automation systems that financial services companies can deploy today — not in some theoretical future. Our co-founders scaled MyBambu to 1.2M+ accounts ($3B+ in money moved), served as CGO at Payphone ($309M TPV), and built compliance programs managing 70+ professionals across 50+ state licenses. We don't advise from the outside. We've been inside the machine.

BODY EMPHASIS — Inter Medium 500

When you need to emphasize key concepts without using bold, Medium weight provides subtle hierarchy while maintaining readability.

STRONG EMPHASIS — Inter SemiBold 600

For critical callouts, stats, or key takeaways, SemiBold provides strong visual weight while staying within the same type family.

CODE / DATA / TECHNICAL — JetBrains Mono Regular 400
const agent = new ComplianceAgent({
  model: "claude-opus-4",
  context: "KYC verification",
  threshold: 0.95
});

Pairing Rules

DO

Pair Montserrat headlines with Inter body text. This creates clear hierarchy and maintains readability.

Example: "Build AI Agents" (Montserrat 800) → "We deploy production-ready systems for financial services" (Inter 400)

DO

Use JetBrains Mono for all technical content. Code, API endpoints, data tables, technical specs.

Example: POST /api/v1/agents or TPV: $309M

DON'T

Never use Montserrat for body text. It's designed for display sizes only. Long paragraphs in Montserrat are hard to read.

DON'T

Never mix more than 3 typefaces. Stick to Montserrat + Inter + JetBrains Mono. No Arial, no Times New Roman, no Georgia.

Tone of Voice

How Axiom speaks and writes

Brand Personality

Axiom's voice is operationally credible, technically precise, unflinchingly direct, globally fluent, and unapologetically ambitious. We sound like the operator who's been in the trenches — because we have been.

1. Operationally Credible

We've scaled real products to real users. We speak in TPV, accounts, conversion rates — not buzzwords.

DO: "We scaled MyBambu to 1M+ accounts."

DON'T: "We help companies grow."

2. Technically Precise

We call things what they are. AI agents, not "smart assistants." LLMs, not "intelligent systems." Agentic workflows, not "automation magic."

DO: "We build Claude-powered AI agents for KYC compliance."

DON'T: "We leverage cutting-edge AI to transform workflows."

3. Unflinchingly Direct

No corporate jargon. No hedging. We say what we mean. If something doesn't work, we say it doesn't work.

DO: "Most AI consultants give you a roadmap. We give you working code."

DON'T: "We partner with you on your AI journey."

4. Globally Fluent

We operate bilingually (English/Spanish) and understand US + LATAM market nuances. We don't translate — we adapt.

DO: "We know what breaks when you scale cross-border payments in LATAM."

DON'T: "We have international experience."

Bilingual Guidelines

English/Spanish usage framework

Language Strategy

Axiom operates bilingually across English and Spanish markets. We are English-first for brand materials (website, pitch deck, proposals) but culturally fluent in Spanish for LATAM client engagements, social media, and region-specific content.

When to Use Which Language

Context Primary Language Rationale
Website (Global) English Primary audience is US-based fintech operators and investors. Spanish version available via toggle.
Pitch Deck (Investors) English Most VC/investor contexts are English-first, even in LATAM markets.
Client Proposals (US) English US financial institutions operate in English.
Client Proposals (LATAM) Spanish LATAM financial institutions prefer Spanish for internal communications.
LinkedIn (Val's Profile) Bilingual (post-by-post) Mix English and Spanish posts based on topic and audience.
Training Materials Match client language If the client team operates in Spanish, deliver training in Spanish.
Email / DMs Match recipient language Mirror the language the prospect/client uses.

Translation Guidelines (Not Just Direct Translation)

We don't translate word-for-word. We adapt for cultural context.

English

Build. Deploy. Train.

Sharp, three-word imperative verbs. Active voice. Command-oriented.

Spanish

Construye. Despliega. Entrena.

Direct Spanish verbs in imperative form. Maintains parallel structure and rhythm.

English

The AI operating system for financial services

Tech-forward framing. "Operating system" signals infrastructure-level thinking.

Spanish

El sistema operativo de IA para servicios financieros

Direct translation works here. "Sistema operativo" is culturally understood in tech contexts.

Cultural Considerations

  • LATAM is not a monolith. Mexican Spanish ≠ Colombian Spanish ≠ Argentine Spanish. Vocabulary and tone shift by country. Use neutral LATAM Spanish for broad content; adapt for country-specific engagements.
  • Formality varies by market. In Mexico and Colombia, business contexts tend to be more formal (usted). In Argentina, voseo is common even in professional settings. Match the client's tone.
  • Tech terminology often stays in English. "AI agent," "deploy," "API," "LLM" are often used in English even in Spanish conversations. Don't force awkward translations.
  • Numbers and metrics are universal. "$309M TPV" works equally well in English and Spanish contexts. Lead with data.

Founder's Language Preference

Val's primary language preference is Spanish for personal communications and internal team contexts. However, the consulting firm operates in English-first contexts because most AI/tech content, investor materials, and US fintech clients communicate in English. When engaging with LATAM clients or markets, Val naturally switches to Spanish — not as a translation layer, but as a native operating mode.
